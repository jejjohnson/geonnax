    Changelog¶

    0.0.7 (2026-08-21)¶

    Features¶

    • deterministic NN building blocks for the pyrox stack (#30) (#40) (de87394)

    0.0.6 (2026-08-20)¶

    Features¶

    • encoders: add GeoContextEncoder for lat/lon/time/covariate context vectors (#38) (359be95)

    0.0.5 (2026-06-08)¶

    Features¶

    • basis: public basis surface + EOF, divergence-free, geodesic-RBF & wavelet bases (#25) (9f160d3)

    0.0.4 (2026-06-07)¶

    Features¶

    • basis: localized RBF, overcomplete Gabor frame, and Gaussian time window (#22) (e3a0d57)
    • mswt: add wavelet-domain attention and the Multi-Scale Wavelet Transformer (#21) (1431af2)
    • spherical-wavelets: scale-discretised wavelet transform on the sphere (#16) (da7616f)
    • unet: thread optional dropout through U-Net blocks for MC-dropout (#20) (3675051)
    • wno: discrete wavelet transform layers and Wavelet Neural Operator (#15) (0fca52e)

    0.0.3 (2026-06-01)¶

    Features¶

    • fno: add Fourier and Spherical Neural Operators (#9) (39f92bf)
    • unet: add dimension-flexible U-Net and reusable conv layers (#7) (b3eeffb)
